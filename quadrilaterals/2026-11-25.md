# Geometry practice – November 25, 2026 (Quadrilaterals)

1. **Warm-up:** Each interior angle of a regular polygon measures 156°. How many sides does the polygon have?
2. **Medium (coordinates):** A(0, 0), B(5, 0), C(8, 4), D(3, 4). Prove ABCD is a rhombus, show it is NOT a square, and find its area.
3. **Challenge (proof):** Rhombus ABCD has diagonals AC and BD intersecting at E. Prove AC ⊥ BD. (Hint: use the fact that a rhombus is a parallelogram, so its diagonals bisect each other, then find congruent triangles.)

<details><summary>Answer key</summary>

1. Each exterior angle = 180° − 156° = 24°. Exterior angles of a convex polygon sum to 360°, so n = 360/24 = **15 sides**.
2. AB = 5 and DC = 5 (both horizontal), AB ∥ DC, so ABCD is a parallelogram (one pair of opposite sides both ∥ and ≅). AD = √(3² + 4²) = 5, so adjacent sides AB ≅ AD, and a parallelogram with two congruent adjacent sides is a **rhombus**. Slope AB = 0 but slope AD = 4/3, so AB is not ⊥ AD: no right angle, so **not a square**. Area = base × height = 5 × 4 = **20** (check: diagonals AC = √80, BD = √20, so ½·√80·√20 = ½·40 = 20).
3. Statements/reasons: (1) AB ≅ AD — sides of a rhombus are congruent. (2) BE ≅ DE — diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other. (3) AE ≅ AE — reflexive property. (4) △ABE ≅ △ADE — SSS. (5) ∠AEB ≅ ∠AED — CPCTC. (6) ∠AEB and ∠AED form a linear pair, so they sum to 180°; being congruent, each is 90°. (7) Therefore **AC ⊥ BD**.

</details>
