# Geometry practice – November 23, 2026 (Quadrilaterals)

1. **Warm-up:** In trapezoid ABCD with AB ∥ CD, m∠A = (4x + 10)° and m∠D = (2x + 20)°. Find x, m∠A, and m∠D.
2. **Medium (algebra-in-figure):** Kite ABCD has AB = AD = 10 and CB = CD = 17. Diagonal BD = 16 and the diagonals intersect at E. Find the length of diagonal AC.
3. **Challenge (coordinates):** A(0, 0), B(6, 0), C(5, 3), D(1, 3). Prove ABCD is an isosceles trapezoid (not a parallelogram), show its diagonals are congruent, and find its area.

<details><summary>Answer key</summary>

1. AB ∥ CD, so ∠A and ∠D are consecutive interior angles (supplementary): (4x + 10) + (2x + 20) = 180, so 6x = 150 and **x = 25**. m∠A = **110°**, m∠D = **70°**.
2. A kite's diagonals are perpendicular, and the diagonal from the vertex of the congruent sides bisects the other diagonal (AC is the axis of symmetry), so BE = 8 and ∠AEB = ∠CEB = 90°. △AEB: AE = √(10² − 8²) = 6. △CEB: CE = √(17² − 8²) = 15. AC = 6 + 15 = **21**.
3. Slope DC = 0 = slope AB, so DC ∥ AB. Slope AD = 3 and slope BC = (3−0)/(5−6) = −3, so AD is not ∥ BC; exactly one pair of parallel sides means it is a trapezoid (not a parallelogram). AD = √(1+9) = √10 and BC = √(1+9) = √10, so the legs are congruent: **isosceles trapezoid**. AC = √(25+9) = √34 and BD = √(25+9) = √34, so **AC ≅ BD**. Area = ½(AB + DC)·h = ½(6 + 4)·3 = **15**.

</details>
