# Geometry practice – November 22, 2026 (Quadrilaterals)

1. **Warm-up:** Find the measure of each interior angle of a regular 12-gon, and the measure of each exterior angle.
2. **Medium (coordinates):** A(0, 0), B(4, 2), C(3, 4), D(−1, 2). Prove ABCD is a rectangle. Is it also a square? Then find its area.
3. **Challenge (proof):** Rectangle ABCD has diagonals AC and BD. Prove AC ≅ BD. (Hint: look for two congruent triangles that share a side.)

<details><summary>Answer key</summary>

1. Each interior angle = (12−2)·180°/12 = **150°**; each exterior angle = 360°/12 = **30°**.
2. Slope AB = 1/2 = slope DC, slope BC = −2 = slope AD, so both pairs of opposite sides are ∥ and ABCD is a parallelogram. (1/2)(−2) = −1, so AB ⊥ BC, giving a right angle, so it is a **rectangle**. AB = √20 and BC = √5 are unequal, so it is **not a square**. Area = √20·√5 = **10**.
3. In rectangle ABCD, BC ≅ AD (opposite sides of a parallelogram), ∠ABC ≅ ∠BAD (both right angles), and AB ≅ AB (reflexive). So △ABC ≅ △BAD by SAS, and **AC ≅ BD** by CPCTC.

</details>
