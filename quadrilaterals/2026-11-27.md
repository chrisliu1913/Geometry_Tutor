# Geometry practice – November 27, 2026 (Quadrilaterals)

1. **Warm-up (algebra-in-figure):** Isosceles trapezoid ABCD has AB ∥ CD, with m∠A = (5x + 5)° and m∠B = (7x − 25)° (∠A and ∠B are base angles on base AB). Find x, m∠A, m∠B, m∠C, and m∠D.
2. **Medium (coordinates):** A(0, 0), B(4, 1), C(6, 6), D(1, 4). Prove ABCD is a kite but NOT a rhombus, show its diagonals are perpendicular, and find its area.
3. **Challenge (proof):** In parallelogram ABCD, diagonals AC and BD are congruent. Prove ABCD is a rectangle. (Hint: use △ABC and △DCB, then think about consecutive angles.)

<details><summary>Answer key</summary>

1. Base angles of an isosceles trapezoid are congruent: 5x + 5 = 7x − 25, so 2x = 30 and **x = 15**. m∠A = **80°**, m∠B = **80°**. Each base angle on AB is supplementary to the one on CD (consecutive interior angles, AB ∥ CD), so m∠C = m∠D = **100°**.
2. AB = √(16+1) = √17 and AD = √(1+16) = √17; CB = √(4+25) = √29 and CD = √(25+4) = √29. Two pairs of adjacent congruent sides, so it is a **kite**; √17 ≠ √29, so not all sides are equal: **not a rhombus**. Slope AC = 1 and slope BD = (4−1)/(1−4) = −1; product −1, so **AC ⊥ BD**. AC = 6√2, BD = 3√2, Area = ½·d₁·d₂ = ½·6√2·3√2 = **18**.
3. (1) AB ≅ DC — opposite sides of a parallelogram. (2) BC ≅ CB — reflexive. (3) AC ≅ DB — given. (4) △ABC ≅ △DCB — SSS. (5) ∠ABC ≅ ∠DCB — CPCTC. (6) AB ∥ DC, so ∠ABC and ∠DCB are consecutive interior angles, supplementary: sum 180°. (7) Congruent and supplementary, so each is 90°. (8) A parallelogram with a right angle is a **rectangle**.
